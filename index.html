Physicochemical Feature Dictionary

Descriptions, units, and ranges for all 11 laboratory inputs.

# Feature Name Standard Unit Observed Range Chemical Role in Wine Quality
01 Fixed Acidity g/dm³ 4.6 – 15.9 Non-volatile organic acids (predominantly tartaric) providing tartness and structural stability.
02 Volatile Acidity g/dm³ 0.12 – 1.58 Acetic acid content. High concentrations produce an unpleasant vinegar taste and lower wine quality.
03 Citric Acid g/dm³ 0.00 – 1.00 Found in small amounts; imparts pleasant freshness and citrus aromatics to the flavor profile.
04 Residual Sugar g/dm³ 0.9 – 15.5 Natural sugar remaining after fermentation completes. Influences sweetness and mouthfeel.
05 Chlorides g/dm³ 0.012 – 0.611 Sodium chloride content (salinity); higher levels adversely affect overall perception.
06 Free Sulfur Dioxide mg/dm³ 1 – 72 Dissolved SO₂ gas; essential for protecting wine against oxidation and microbial growth.
07 Total Sulfur Dioxide mg/dm³ 6 – 289 Sum of free and bound SO₂; excessive levels cause harsh pungent aroma and sulfur smell.
08 Density g/cm³ 0.990 – 1.004 Specific density governed primarily by alcohol content (lighter) and residual sugar (heavier).
09 pH scale 2.74 – 4.01 Standard logarithmic acidity scale (typical range 3.2 – 3.6). Governs antimicrobial preservation.
10 Sulphates g/dm³ 0.33 – 2.00 Potassium sulphate salt additive serving as an antioxidant and antimicrobial stabilizer.
11 Alcohol % vol 8.4 – 14.9 Ethanol volume percentage; empirical strongest positive driver of wine quality ratings.

Supervised Learning Models

Comparison of evaluated classification algorithms trained on the UCI wine quality benchmark.

Best Model Selected Ensemble Method

Random Forest

100 decision trees trained with bootstrap aggregating and random feature subsets.

Accuracy 90.3%
Precision 87.5%
Recall 84.1%
F1 Score 85.7%
Selection Rationale: Mitigates individual tree overfitting, robust against correlated features, and delivers the highest harmonic F1 score across classes.
Benchmark Kernel Classifier

Support Vector Machine

Radial Basis Function (RBF) kernel with standard feature scaling optimizing max-margin hyperplanes.

Accuracy 86.8%
Precision 81.2%
Recall 77.5%
F1 Score 79.3%
Characteristics: Effective in high-dimensional continuous space, but lower recall on non-linear borderline quality boundaries.
Benchmark Neural Network

MLP Classifier

Multi-layer perceptron neural network with hidden layers, ReLU activations, and Adam optimization.

Accuracy 85.1%
Precision 79.4%
Recall 75.8%
F1 Score 77.5%
Characteristics: Capable of non-linear parameter modeling, but requires larger sample depth to avoid early plateauing.

Project Overview

Summary of project objectives, machine learning workflow, and software technologies.

Project Purpose
“Classify wine into quality categories using physicochemical properties.”

Evaluates how analytical laboratory measurements (such as alcohol, volatile acidity, density, and sulphates) reliably predict wine quality grades without requiring subjective human sensory tests.
